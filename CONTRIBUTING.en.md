# Share a use or improvement

[简体中文](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Home](README.md)

You can improve the skill in three concrete ways: contribute a new use, correct a case fact, or improve a translation. You do not need to reveal your identity or a complete private experience.

## Contribute a situation or its progress

In a repository Issue or Pull Request, include:

- The situation without names, employers, or contact information, preferably rewritten as fiction. Explain what should change and what would count as improvement.
- A fact that affects the decision, and whether the action has the required authority, resources, and voluntary participation.
- If action has been taken, what happened, the next obstacle, and what remains unresolved.
- The adjustment you suggest to a question, judgment, conversation, action, or closure check.

For example: “The advice asks a colleague to cover a shift, but he cannot authorize scheduling. The manager approved a two-week trial without any later arrangement, so the long-term scheduling problem is still unresolved.” This maps feedback to a specific judgment and preserves actual progress without publishing a full conversation.

A new route can help, as can making an ordinary solution workable, responding to feedback, or identifying what remains unresolved. Label designed demonstrations as fictional, and distinguish them from a user’s actual experience.

## Correct a case or translation

Give the case ID, such as L7, the specific sentence, and a verifiable source. For translation changes, check the corresponding language too, preserving action attribution, outcomes, conditions, and source links. Matching case IDs refer to the same event in both languages. When expanding a journey, distinguish what the story shows, research interpretation, and real-life application. Keep missing outcomes unknown; one agreement does not establish lasting success.

Do not upload complete scripts, show stills, or actor portraits. Do not upload private chats without permission. Submit only material you have the right to license, and identify third-party sources.

## Contribution licenses

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise before acceptance, contributions intended for inclusion use the corresponding existing license: CC BY 4.0 for original content and MIT for scripts. See the [licenses](LICENSE.en.md) and [third-party notice](NOTICE.en.md) for scope.

## Keep release material aligned

Update both language cores and guides when runtime behavior changes, and register new documents in the [language map](LANGUAGE_PARITY.json). Preserve the distinction between bounded initial clarification and continuing with execution feedback, and explain how outcomes are checked. Confirm changes to goals or completion criteria. Do not promise automatic memory, reminders, or external execution that the Skill itself cannot supply.

Run `python3 scripts/check_package.py` before submission. Exclude machine paths, session logs, model credentials, adapters, and abandoned videos.
