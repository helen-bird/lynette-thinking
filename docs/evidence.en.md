The same scene can appear in several references to explain different parts of a decision. Actions proposed by other characters remain attributed to them: for example, Renee supplies the accepted office design in S7E20.

## Reading the sources

Sources include public unofficial transcripts, scene descriptions, episode summaries, and official release pages. Links appear alongside the relevant cases. A summary can support a storyline without supporting the exact wording of a conversation; differences that affect interpretation are retained.

One agreement does not establish lasting success, and unshown events remain unknown. Her mistakes, other people's choices, and later consequences are part of the material. A real-world recommendation must still fit the user's authority, resources, preferences, and relationships.

For a correction, provide the episode, the sentence to change, and a source that supports the change. See [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.en.md).
